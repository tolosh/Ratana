# Combine patient record navigation

## Goal
Show one **Patient records** entry in the clinician menu without removing either the records list or individual clinical record screens.

## Changes
- Remove the duplicate **Patient record** menu entry.
- Keep **Patient records** opening the searchable patient records list.
- Keep individual patient clinical records accessible from the network dashboard and clinical queue.
- Highlight **Patient records** in the menu while viewing an individual record, alert, or handover.

## Validation
- Confirm only one patient-record menu item appears on desktop and mobile.
- Confirm the records list and patient drill-downs remain accessible.
